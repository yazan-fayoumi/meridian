Not another
sunglasses brand.

Most eyewear brands start with a factory catalogue and work backwards into a story. We're doing the opposite — starting from a point of view about light, material, and how a frame should sit on a face, then sourcing to match it, one factory relationship at a time.

Meridian is based in Amman and built for people who want their first pair to already look like a decision, not an impulse buy. No filler colourways, no drop culture — a small, considered collection that grows slowly.

I.

Sourced, not stocked

Every frame is chosen from eyewear-specialist manufacturers and tested before it earns the Meridian mark.

II.

Few, not many

A tight lineup on purpose. We'd rather perfect five frames than dilute across fifty.

III.

Made to be worn at noon

Designed around real Jordanian and Gulf light — glare, heat, and the sun directly overhead.

Sourced, not stocked

Every frame comes from a vetted eyewear-specialist manufacturer — no generic dropship catalogue.

Shipped from Amman

Once live, orders across Jordan and the wider Gulf ship directly from our own stock — no long wait on a factory order.

Small first-run batches

Early collections are produced in limited quantities while we get fit and finish right, not mass-run from day one.

A few questions
we hear early.

When does the first collection launch?+

We're finalising frames with our manufacturing partner now. Waitlist members get access before the collection goes public — join below and we'll email you directly.

Will you ship outside Jordan?+

Yes — our first priority is Jordan and the Gulf, with wider shipping added shortly after launch. Waitlist members will hear about coverage first.

What materials are you using?+

Our first frames are acetate, chosen for weight and finish over cheaper injected plastic. Lens options (polarized and UV400 as standard) are still being finalised with our supplier.

Can I get updates without committing to buy?+

Of course — the waitlist is just for updates on the launch. No account or payment needed until the collection is actually live.
