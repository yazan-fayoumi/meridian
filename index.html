The house

Not another sunglasses brand.

Most eyewear brands start with a factory catalogue and work backwards into a story. We are doing the opposite—starting from a point of view about light, material, and how a frame should sit on a face, then sourcing to match it, one factory relationship at a time.

Meridian is based in Amman and built for people who want their first pair to already look like a decision, not an impulse buy. No filler colourways, no drop culture—a small, considered collection that grows slowly.

II.

III.

Made for noon

Designed around real Jordanian and Gulf light—glare, heat, and the sun directly overhead.

Questions

A few things we hear early.

When does the first collection launch?+

We are finalising frames with our manufacturing partner now. Waitlist members get access before the collection goes public.

Will you ship outside Jordan?+

Yes. Our first priority is Jordan and the Gulf, with wider shipping added after launch. Waitlist members will hear about coverage first.

What materials are you using?+

Our first frames are acetate, chosen for weight and finish over cheaper injected plastic. Lens options are being finalised with our supplier.

Can I get updates without committing to buy?+

Of course. The waitlist is simply for launch updates—no account or payment is needed until the collection is actually live.
